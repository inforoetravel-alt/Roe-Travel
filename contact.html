FAQ

Common questions

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Most fares and rooms show their change and cancellation terms before you pay. If you need to change a confirmed booking, email us with your reference and we'll walk you through the options and any fee.

Why did the price change between search and checkout?

It shouldn't. Roe Travel shows the full price, including taxes and fees, at search. If you ever see a different total at checkout, tell support — that's exactly the kind of thing we fix.

What payment methods are accepted?

Major debit and credit cards are accepted at checkout. Payment details are encrypted in transit and are never stored on our servers.

My flight was changed or cancelled by the airline — what now?

Email support with your booking reference as soon as you know. We'll help you rebook, get a refund, or find the next best option depending on the airline's policy.

Do you cover destinations outside Europe?

Yes — Roe Travel lists flights and hotels across 29 countries, from short regional hops to long-haul and multi-city trips.
